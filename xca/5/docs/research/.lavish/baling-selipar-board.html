Baling Selipar - research and gamification

48-hour jam. Godot 4.6 web build for GamePix. Landscape. Top-down. Solo vs bots, 3v3. Primitive shapes only.

1. Research summary

Traditional rules

  • 3 slippers stand upright in a triangle. One more slipper is thrown.
  • Two sides: throwers and defenders. Size varies, often 4-6.
  • Each thrower gets 3 throws. A miss passes the turn.
  • A knock-down flips roles. Throwers now hit defenders. A hit defender is "mati" (out).
  • Defenders rebuild the stack. Win rules conflict between sources.
  • Distance is not standard. One group used 3 m, then 4 m.
  • Fouls: stay behind the line, no body contact, no chasing with a slipper.

Confidence is medium. Sources are blogs and student papers.

Culture and names

  • Also called tuju selipar and susun selipar.
  • Kampung childhood play. Needs only a yard and a chalk line.
  • Revived at traditional-games events and campus culture days.
  • Close cousin: baling tin. Ball plus 15 tins. A rebuild scores 1 point.
  • Batu seremban adds only one idea: levels of rising difficulty.

2. Concepts side by side

Recommended

A. Susun Tumbang (stack siege)

Loop. Each team guards a 3-sandal stack. Throw to topple the enemy stack. A topple scores. The owner rebuilds. A hit rebuilder is mati.

Round. 3 min, 3v3. 3 sandals per player. Pick fallen sandals up by walking over them.

Win. Most topples at the buzzer. First to 5 ends early. Tie goes to sudden death.

Powers.

  • Angin Kencang - curve throw, 10 s
  • Selipar Kembar - 3-sandal fan, 14 s
  • Tapak Besi - block one hit, 18 s
  • Larian Kilat - sprint, 8 s
  • Pulang - recall all sandals, 20 s
  • Tin Palsu - decoy stack, 25 s

AI. Roles: striker, guard, rebuilder. Aim error and reaction delay set the difficulty.

Mobile feel. Aim line shows the reverse direction. Length is power. Haptic tick on release.

First 30 s. You throw, a stack topples, a bot runs to rebuild.

Risks. Topple feel. Slow rebuild pacing.

B. Pasar Malam Mati (tag-out)

Loop. No stack. Hit enemies to mark them mati. Ammo piles respawn mid-field.

Round. 2 min, 2v2.

Win. Most mati hits.

Powers. Same pool as A, minus Tin Palsu.

AI. Chase the nearest enemy, throw in range.

Mobile feel. Same drag throw.

First 30 s. Instant hits.

Risks. Looks like generic dodgeball. Loses the stack, so recognition drops.

C. Tendang Tin (stylized soccer)

Loop. A heavy tin sits mid-field. Sandal hits push it. Push it into the enemy gate.

Round. 3 min, 3v3.

Win. Most goals.

Powers. Same pool plus magnet.

AI. Push the tin toward the gate. Defend the gate.

Mobile feel. Drag throw becomes a push shot.

First 30 s. Readable at once.

Risks. Tin physics on web. Sandals stop being a knock-down game.

3. Arena and mobile controls (concept A)

  • Left joystick moves. Right thumb drags back from any point in the zone.
  • Drag length sets power. The throw goes the opposite way. The dotted line previews it.
  • Desktop: WASD moves. Mouse drag from the player does the same pull.
  • Each team has a base and a stack. The chalk line marks the limit for rebuilders.

4. Recommendation

Build concept A, Susun Tumbang. It keeps the stack, the three throws, the rebuild and mati, so Malaysian players recognise the game at once. It fits 3v3 and short rounds.

Feasibility 7 of 10 for 48 h Rules are simple. Touch polish and AI take the time.

5. Scope

Build first (core loop)

  1. Arena, chalk line, 2 stacks of 3 sandals.
  2. Move with joystick or WASD.
  3. Drag-back throw with aim line.
  4. Topple check, score, timer, win screen.
  5. Pickup and 3-sandal ammo belt.
  6. Bots: one thrower, one rebuilder.
  7. 2 powers, after the loop is fun.

Cut

  • Powers beyond 2
  • Online play
  • Extra maps and skins
  • Custom art and layered music
  • Tutorial beyond one text card
  • 2v2 and 3v3 toggle
