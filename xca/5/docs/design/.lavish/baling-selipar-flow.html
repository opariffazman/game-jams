Baling Selipar - round flow

Attackers throw sandals at the stack. If it falls they run, and the defenders hit them with a passed sandal. Roles swap every round. Annotate anything that is wrong.

Attackers (circle) Defenders (square) Dashed box or line: not decided yet

Topple, top-down

Numbers show throw order. The drag-back on attacker one charges the throw. Attacker three misses.

Scatter, top-down

One sandal circles the defenders. The ring on the holder marks who has it. One attacker is already mati.

Phone controls, landscape

One drag-and-release gesture does every throw: attacker throw, defender pass, defender hit.

Attacker mechanics

One drag-and-release gesture stays the only throw input. Everything below adds depth on top of it.

Topple phase: four ways to throw

Drag length sets power. Drag bend sets curve. Flat and lob share the same drag, and the drag length picks between them.

Leftover sandals

A miss stays on the field. The next attacker can hit it into the stack.

Near-miss meter

A close dodge fills a segment. A full meter charges the superpower.

Scatter phase: run and rebuild

Dash is invulnerable for a short window, then goes on cooldown.

Carrying slows the attacker. Holding at the spot fills the ring.

Pass-lane steal (not decided)

An attacker who cuts the line could take the sandal. This is the biggest swing in a round.

Defender mechanics and playing both sides

You play one seat per round. Two bots fill the other seats on your team. The roles swap every round, so you play both sides.

Your seat each round

The ring marks the character you steer. In round 2 you are an attacker and wait for your turn in the throw queue.

Defender actions

Block and orbit work during the topple phase. Kick away and pick up work during the scatter phase.

Pass

The same drag-and-release gesture. The sandal snaps to a teammate inside the cone and curves over an attacker.

Hit with lead aim

The throw aims at where the attacker will be, so a defender does not need perfect timing on a phone.

Pass chain

Each pass adds speed to the next throw. A fast chain beats a dash.

Control follows the sandal

After a pass you steer the receiver, so you always act on the sandal.

Rules as I read them

  • Attackers throw one sandal each, in turn, at the centre stack.
  • Defenders own the stack and stand around it. They can move, they do not throw in this phase.
  • Sandals are physics bodies. A hit pushes them. The stack is down when a sandal leaves its spot.
  • Throws used up with the stack still standing: every attacker is mati. Roles swap.
  • Stack down: attackers scatter, defenders try to stop the rebuild.
  • Defenders share one sandal like a handball. They pass it, and a thrown sandal that hits an attacker makes that attacker mati.
  • Mati has no respawn. All attackers mati: defenders win the round and roles swap.
  • No timer is described. The rebuild needs an explicit end rule (see question list).
