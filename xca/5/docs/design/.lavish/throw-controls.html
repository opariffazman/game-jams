Throw controls - research

You said the throw feels unintuitive. This board shows why, compares four schemes, and recommends one. Annotate anything that is wrong, then answer the three questions at the bottom.

Why it feels unintuitive today

  • The aim line starts at the thumb, so it never touches the thrower. (throw_pad.gd:79)
  • The throw goes opposite to the drag, so the player reverses it in their head. (throw_pad.gd:146)
  • The target is picked silently on release. One drag can pass, hit, or miss. (character.gd:208-219)
  • No cancel area is drawn, so a player cannot tell how to back out.

All four come from reading the code. One playtest note exists: "a bit unintuitive". Other causes are unconfirmed.

Recommended phone layout

  • Fire button at least 72 px. Dash button at least 56 px. At least 24 px between them.
  • Drag slop 16 px. Lock cone 40 degrees. A new target must beat the old one by 8 degrees. These values are unconfirmed on a device.
  • A ghost hand taps the fire button the first time. It replays after 8 seconds idle, up to 3 times.
  • Haptic pulse on lock change and fire, on Chrome for Android only. Safari does not support it.
  • Desktop: click fires. Hold and move to pick. Right click or Escape cancels. Q and E cycle, F fires.

Friend and foe cues

Friends: blue, 8.3 to 1 on the floor Foes: red, 5.8 to 1 on the floor

Blue and red differ by only 1.4 to 1 in brightness, so shape and ring style carry the side too. A blocked lane uses a cross icon, so colour is never the only cue.

Accessibility rules checked

  • WCAG 2.5.7 Dragging Movements (AA): a dragged function needs a way to work without dragging. Scheme D passes with a tap. W3C
  • WCAG 2.5.1 Pointer Gestures (A): does not apply, because a drag in any direction is not path-based. W3C
  • WCAG 2.5.8 Target Size (AA): 24 by 24 px minimum. W3C
  • Game Accessibility Guidelines: large virtual controls, a simpler alternative, assist modes such as auto-aim. Full list
  • Xbox Accessibility Guideline 107: alternative input with the same function. Microsoft
