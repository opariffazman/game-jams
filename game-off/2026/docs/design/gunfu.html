01 / Research

What makes a clip worth sharing

The design trick: simple input, rich output

Superhot proves the pattern. Time moves only when you move, so one small input produces a big cinematic result. Reviewers call it more puzzle than shooter. That also makes it playable by anyone.

Sources: Superhot on Wikipedia, Giant Bomb analysis of its time mechanic.

Reel anatomy (my judgment, not sourced)

  • Silhouette: readable at phone size in one frame.
  • Speed ramp: real speed, then a dip to slow-mo, then a snap back on the hit.
  • Money shot: one clear kill moment every 5 to 8 seconds.
  • Clean frame: reel mode hides all UI.
  • Loop length: 15 to 30 seconds, ends where it starts.
  • Beat sync: hits land on the music.

Godot and web facts found

  • Slow-mo is Engine.time_scale. Audio does not follow it, so set AudioServer.playback_speed_scale too. Tween the ramp, never snap it.
  • Shaders that read TIME slow down with Engine.time_scale. Plan for that in glow and scanline shaders.
  • JavaScriptBridge.eval reaches the page canvas on web builds.
  • Sharing a file uses the Web Share API: call navigator.canShare(), then navigator.share() inside a tap. It needs HTTPS. An iframe needs allow="web-share".

Sources: Godot forum slow-motion thread, Godot 4 bullet-time addon, Godot web export docs, web.dev Web Share guide.

Unconfirmed and risky

  • In-game recording: canvas.captureStream() into MediaRecorder is plausible. No source confirms it in a Godot web build. Test it on day one.
  • itch.io iframe: unknown whether it sets allow="web-share". Plan a download fallback.
  • iPhone: the Godot 4.1 docs say web exports do not run on iOS. Newer versions probably fix this. Check on a real iPhone with the current Godot version before committing.
  • Fallback that always works: a clean Reel Mode that players screen-record themselves.

02 / Shared reel engine

Every proposal gets the same showcase pipeline

Look
Neon noir. Black world, flat silhouettes, one magenta rim light, one cyan rim light, amber muzzle flash. Silhouettes keep animation cheap and read well on a phone.
Animation
Pose-to-pose. Six to eight poses per move, smear arcs between them, a 60 ms hit stop on impact. Stick limbs drawn with Line2D avoid a full rig.
Juice
Camera2D punch zoom, bullet trails with Line2D, shell casings, a chromatic aberration screen shader, and a title card at the end.
Flash-safe
Muzzle flashes cover a small area and never repeat more than three times per second. A toggle removes flashes and screen shake. Slow-mo helps readability for everyone.
Content
Original hitman character and original music. No film name, likeness, or soundtrack. Enemies drop as silhouettes with no blood, which also keeps the itch.io page easy to tag.

03 / Proposals

Five gun-fu games

G1 / bullet-time dance

Time Thumb

Your thumb is the clock. Drag and time runs. Lift and the room freezes with bullets hanging in the air. Steer the hitman through the frozen storm. He auto-fires, flips, and disarms anything he passes.

Room freezes→Read bullets→Drag a path→Auto strikes→Room clear→Reel replay
Thumb
Drag anywhere in the bottom zone. Drag speed sets time speed. Lifting stops time. No second input.
Cool
Frozen bullets with glowing trails make a great still frame. The replay plays the same run at real speed, then ramps down at each kill.
SHIFT
Shift time. Slow, stop, and rewind it with your thumb.
Loops
Seconds: read and drag. Minutes: one room. Days: daily room and style-rank medals.
Access
No reflex needed. The player controls the pace, so it suits slower players. A limit on drag speed removes motion spikes.
Godot
Set Engine.time_scale from drag speed with a floor of zero. Bullets are simple Area2D nodes. Safe build.
Risk
A close cousin of Superhot. Needs its own twist. The Hot Swap proposal supplies one.

G2 / tactical gun-fu puzzle

Hot Swap

The room freezes. Every gunman telegraphs a shot line. Tap one enemy and you swap places with that enemy. You land with a move: a disarm, a throw, a shot. Their friends are still firing, and the lines now cross the wrong body. Clear the room in the fewest swaps.

Frozen room→Read shot lines→Tap to swap→1 s slow-mo ballet→Freeze again→Reel replay
Thumb
One tap on a target. Targets are large circles in the bottom two thirds of the screen. A left or right lane layout moves the room for one-thumb reach.
Cool
Each swap triggers a hand-timed move with a speed ramp. Enemies shoot each other, so the reel shows tidy chain kills. The end replay cuts between three camera angles.
SHIFT
Shift positions. Swap charges are the resource. Fewer swaps earn more stars.
Loops
Seconds: read, tap, watch. Minutes: one room, one to two minutes. Days: daily room plus a share code made of the tap list.
Access
Turn-based with no timer. Shot lines use dash patterns and arrows, not only color. A preview ghost shows the outcome before a tap commits (optional).
Godot
Fully deterministic. The tap list is the whole replay, so the reel engine is easy. Needs enemy types: pistol, shotgun cone, shield, knife.
Risk
Level design needs care. Ten rooms is enough. A hand-timed move per enemy type costs animation time.

G3 / timeline choreography

Echo Kata

Record take one. Take two plays take one as a ghost while you record a second hitman. Take three adds a third. The room is cleared by three of you in a single synchronized scene. The final replay is the shareable fight scene.

Record take→Ghosts replay→Add a take→All three sync→Director cut
Thumb
Drag a path and tap to strike during each take. A take is 6 seconds. Hold-to-toggle options replace any sustained hold.
Cool
The strongest reel moment of the five. Three colored hitmen crossing paths in one clean shot looks like a choreographer's cut.
SHIFT
Shift time. You shift between takes, past and present.
Loops
Seconds: one take. Minutes: one three-take scene. Days: daily room.
Access
Takes are untimed to start. Ghost colors also differ by trail pattern. Slow mode doubles take length.
Godot
Needs a fixed-step deterministic sim and an input recorder. Replays must match to the frame.
Risk
High. A timing drift breaks the whole design. Strong stretch goal, risky as the main game.

G4 / rhythm gun-fu

Kata Beat

Hitting notes on the beat performs a gun-kata routine on the top half of the screen. Each lane fires a different move. A perfect streak chains moves into a long unbroken combo. A miss stumbles the hero, not the music.

Notes fall→Tap lane on beat→Move plays→Combo builds→Finisher
Thumb
Three large pads in the bottom zone. One thumb covers all three. A single-lane mode merges them for one-button play.
Cool
Beat-synced choreography, finishers, and a strong music hook. Easy to cut into reels because the music already sets the rhythm.
SHIFT
Shift gears. Each lane is a different move or weapon. Gear changes accelerate the tempo.
Loops
Seconds: one note. Minutes: one track. Days: new track and a score board.
Access
Weakest here. Rhythm needs timing precision. Offer wide windows, auto-play assist, and a latency offset slider. Browser audio latency varies.
Godot
Chart data as a Resource. Audio sync on web needs a calibration screen.
Risk
Needs original music and a chart per track. Weakest tie to SHIFT.

G5 / boss duel

Duel Shift

A one-on-one duel in a neon hallway. The boss telegraphs a shot. Shift your stance to dodge. A clean dodge opens a counter, and the hero chains a cinematic takedown. Each boss has a new tell pattern.

Boss tells→Shift stance→Dodge→Counter strike→Phase change→Takedown
Thumb
Three stance pads, or swipe left, down, right. One tap per beat of the duel.
Cool
Boss intro cards, close-ups, and a fixed takedown for each boss. Great for a trailer, weaker as a sharable run.
SHIFT
Shift stance. Tell patterns also shift between phases.
Loops
Seconds: tell and dodge. Minutes: one boss. Days: boss rush with a daily modifier.
Access
Tells use shape, icon, and sound. A slow mode lengthens each tell. A no-fail mode replays a round instead of ending the duel.
Godot
State machine per boss. Medium animation cost, because each boss needs several unique poses.
Risk
Each new boss costs a lot. Three bosses are the floor. Low variety inside a boss.

05 / Recommendation

Build Hot Swap, shown in Time Thumb style

Hot Swap makes the player's input one tap that picks a target. The outcome is fully deterministic. That makes the reel engine simple, because the tap list is the replay. It also needs no timer, so it is the most accessible. The world stays frozen until a tap, so the look borrows the best part of Time Thumb: bullets hang in the air while you plan.

  • Week 1: One room, silhouette hero, swap move, frozen planning, slow-mo ballet. Go or no-go on feel.
  • Week 2: Four enemy types, ten rooms, swap par and stars. Start the music.
  • Week 3: Reel engine: deterministic replay, camera director, speed ramps, title card, share code. Test recording and Web Share on real phones.
  • Week 4: Daily room, assist toggles, flash-safe mode, device testing, itch.io page, trailer reel.

Echo Kata is a stretch goal. It reuses the same deterministic replay. Kata Beat and Duel Shift cost more content than they return for a solo jam.
